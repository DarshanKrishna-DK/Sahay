From need to order

Keep serving customers at the counter. Sahay talks to sellers, bargains, and waits for your yes.

  1. Step 1

    Say what you need

    Speak the way you talk to a supplier. No menus, no item codes.

  2. Step 2

    Sahay finds sellers

    Real sellers who serve your city. If a source is down, Sahay says so.

  3. Step 3

    It bargains

    It contacts sellers near you, says first that it is an automated assistant, and asks for a better price.

  4. Step 4

    Your yes

    Sahay reads the deal out loud. Say yes or tap Yes; only then does it order.

Questions

What shop owners ask first.

?
01Is Sahay a person?

No. Sahay is an automated assistant, and it says so in the first sentence of every call.

02Can it order without me?

No. No order, bill or payment link is made without your spoken yes or the Yes button. Sahay bargains and prepares the deal; you decide.

03Which languages does it speak?

Talk in Hindi, Kannada or English, or mix them. Sahay answers in the language you spoke, in its own script.

04Where do the sellers come from?

Sellers who serve your city, from IndiaMART listings. If a source is down, Sahay says so; it never makes sellers up.

05Are the payments real?

Not yet. Payments run in Razorpay test mode, and a simulated vendor is always labelled "simulated vendor".

06What do I need to start?

A phone or laptop with a microphone. Open Sahay, sign up with your phone number and a PIN, and say what you need.
