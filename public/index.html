How Sahay buys for your shop in three steps

Keep serving customers at the counter. Sahay talks to sellers, bargains, and waits for your yes.

  1. 01

    Say what you need

    Speak the way you talk to a supplier. No menus, no item codes.

The whole loop, step by step

Eight steps. Sahay does the running around; you say what you need, and you say yes.

  2. Real sellers
  3. Bargaining

  5. Sell and khata
  6. Stock low

  8. Languages

1Speak

Say it the way you say it

Talk in Hindi, Kannada or English, or mix them. Sahay writes your words down as a clear need.

2Real sellers

Sahay finds real sellers near you

Sellers who serve your city, from IndiaMART listings. If a source is down, Sahay says so; it never makes sellers up.

3Bargaining

It bargains, and says who it is

The first sentence of every call says Sahay is an automated assistant. Then it brings the price down, round by round.

“Namaste, I am Sahay, an automated assistant for your shop.”

Example

4Your yes

Nothing is ordered without your yes

Sahay reads the deal out loud. You say yes, or tap Yes. Only then does it order, and your yes goes into the record.

seller 01₹55/kg × 50 kg = ₹2,750

Example deal: seller 01, 55 rupees a kilo, 50 kilos, 2,750 rupees in all.

Example

5Sell and khata

Sell by voice, keep your khata

Say who bought what. Sahay reads the bill back, makes it after your yes, and keeps track of who owes you.

6Stock low

Every bill takes stock out

You set the level for each item. When stock falls below it, Sahay tells you.

7Buy again

It asks before it buys again

Sahay offers the last good deal again. Say yes and the loop starts over; say no and nothing happens.

8Languages

Your language, both ways

Sahay answers in the language you spoke, in its own script. Choose the language for this page here.
